Denial Management FAQs

What is the difference between a claim rejection and a claim denial?

A rejection occurs at the clearinghouse or payer gateway level before the claim enters processing (often due to formatting or demographic errors). A denial occurs after the claim is processed and insurance determines payment will not be issued based on policy, coding, or clinical reasons.

How quickly does Priority Sure initiate action on a newly received denial?

Our denial management team acts on all ERAs and paper EOB denials within 24 to 48 hours of posting, ensuring strict adherence to short payer appeal windows.

How do you prevent the same denials from happening repeatedly?

We conduct monthly root-cause reporting that highlights whether denials originated in eligibility verification, charge entry, or coding. We then update practice front-end workflows and claim scrubber rules to eliminate repeat occurrences.
