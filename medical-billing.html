Medical Billing FAQs

How quickly are claims processed and submitted after patient visits?

Priority Sure enforces a strict 24-hour SLA. Once encounter logs or clinical notes are finalized in your EHR, our team scrubs, verifies, and submits the electronic claims within one business day.

Do you work with primary, secondary, and tertiary insurance payers?

Yes. We manage claim submissions across Commercial plans (Aetna, BCBS, Cigna, UHC), Medicare, Medicaid, Workers' Compensation, Auto PIP, and all secondary/tertiary crossover coordination.

What happens if a claim is rejected at the clearinghouse?

Clearinghouse rejections are flagged instantly in our daily workflow queues. Our specialists correct demographic, coding, or insurance ID errors and resubmit the claim within 24 hours.
