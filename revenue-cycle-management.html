The Challenge with Fragmented Healthcare Billing

Modern medical reimbursement is complex. Between evolving payer guidelines, stringent coding standards, modifier requirements, and shifting patient liability models, managing revenue in-house often leads to backlogged claims, unresolved denials, and delayed cash flow.

Priority Sure’s Full-Cycle Revenue Cycle Management addresses every critical touchpoint in the billing lifecycle. Instead of treating tasks in isolation, our integrated framework syncs front-desk intake, certified medical coding, rapid claim transmission, automated payment posting, and aggressive accounts receivable management.

Full-Cycle RCM FAQs

What is the difference between Medical Billing and Full-Cycle RCM?

Medical billing typically focuses on generating and submitting claims from encounter forms. Full-Cycle RCM encompasses the entire clinical-financial pipeline—starting before the patient is seen (eligibility and prior authorization) through post-encounter coding, payment posting, denial resolution, and longitudinal reporting.

How does Priority Sure charge for Full-Cycle RCM?

We operate on a performance-aligned percentage model based on net collections. You only pay when you get paid, guaranteeing that our team is fully motivated to maximize reimbursement and eliminate underpayments.

How do we track the financial performance of our practice?

Priority Sure provides weekly, monthly, and quarterly transparent reporting, detailing total billing volume, collection rates, net days in A/R, denial reasons, and payer trend analyses.
