Navigating the Complexities of Work Injury Reimbursement

Workers' Compensation billing differs fundamentally from standard commercial insurance. Claims are governed by intricate state-specific fee schedules, mandatory medical report attachments (e.g., Doctor's First Report, PR-2, C-4 forms), and strict pre-authorization rules established by insurance adjustors and nurse case managers.

A single missing narrative report, body-part code mismatch, or unverified claim number can freeze payment for months or trigger an unjust downcoding. Priority Sure's dedicated Workers' Comp specialists manage the entire life cycle—from initial claim verification and adjustor communication to second-bill appeals and fee schedule dispute resolution.

Workers' Compensation Billing FAQs

Can a provider balance bill a Workers' Comp patient?

No. In almost all state Workers' Compensation systems, healthcare providers are prohibited by law from balance billing the injured worker for treatment of an accepted work injury.

What happens if a Workers' Comp claim is denied or contested by the employer?

If a claim is contested, Priority Sure assists in billing the patient's primary group health plan as a backup or placing a formal medical lien against the contested case pending legal resolution.

How does Priority Sure handle state-specific fee schedule updates?

Our billing system updates state fee schedules annually or bi-annually as mandates change, ensuring that your practice always bills the current maximum allowable rate.
