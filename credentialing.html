Provider Credentialing FAQs

How long does medical credentialing usually take?

Commercial payer credentialing typically takes 60 to 90 days, while government plans like Medicare and Medicaid take between 45 and 90 days depending on state processing backlogs.

What happens if a payer network is closed to new providers?

If a carrier panel is closed, Priority Sure submits a formal network exception request, highlighting your practice's unique geographic location, specialized patient services, or multi-language availability to gain access.

Why is CAQH re-attestation so important?

CAQH requires re-attestation every 120 days. Failure to update CAQH profiles causes commercial insurance plans to drop providers from active status, leading to claim denials and payment suspensions.
