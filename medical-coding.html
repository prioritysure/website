Precision Medical Coding Driven by Clinical Integrity

In an era of rigorous payer audits, RAC reviews, and complex NCCI edit guidelines, coding errors do more than delay claims—they expose practices to costly compliance penalties. Priority Sure pairs specialty-trained coders with automated scrubbing systems to capture every billable detail while keeping your practice completely compliant.

Medical Coding FAQs

What credentials do Priority Sure medical coders hold?

All Priority Sure coding professionals hold active credentials from AAPC or AHIMA, including CPC (Certified Professional Coder), COC (Certified Outpatient Coder), CCS (Certified Coding Specialist), or specialty-specific certifications.

How do you handle queries when provider notes are incomplete?

Our team utilizes a structured, non-leading Physician Query Process. If clinical documentation lacks necessary specificity, we issue clear electronic queries to the provider before assigning final codes, preserving compliance and maximum accuracy.

Can you perform a sample coding audit before we commit to ongoing services?

Yes! We offer a complimentary, confidential 10-chart audit for healthcare organizations to assess accuracy rates, identify missing revenue opportunities, and demonstrate our clinical coding proficiency.
