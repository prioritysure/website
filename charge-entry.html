Bridge the Gap Between Clinical Documentation & Claim Submission

Charge entry is the crucial bridge connecting patient care with financial reimbursement. Errors committed during charge entry—such as missing CPT modifiers, mismatched diagnosis pointers, incorrect units, or outdated fee schedules—inevitably result in clearinghouse rejections or payer denials downstream.

At Priority Sure, our dedicated charge entry specialists review every Superbill, EHR charge ticket, and provider note. We reconcile completed provider appointments against billed charges to guarantee zero missing procedures, while applying pre-submission scrubbing to maximize clean claim acceptance.

Our 4-Step Charge Entry Audit Workflow

A structured approach ensuring speed, accuracy, and compliance across every billed claim.

Stage Operational Actions Quality Control Result
1. Encounter Reconciliation Comparing appointment schedules/EHR logs against received Superbills and digital charge tickets. 100% encounter capture; zero unbilled patient visits.
2. Coding & Modifier Audit Cross-referencing CPT codes with clinical chart notes, verifying diagnosis pointer links and required modifiers. Audit-compliant entry with zero bundling or modifier errors.
3. System Data Capture Entering charge codes, units, rendered location codes, ordering NPIs, and fee amounts into Practice Management software. Sub-24 hour turnaround from clinical encounter.

Charge Entry FAQs

How quickly are encounter charges processed after a patient visit?

Priority Sure enforces a strict 24-hour SLA. Charges received or signed off in your EHR by providers today are reviewed, audited, and entered by our charge team within 24 business hours.

How do you catch unbilled encounters or missed hospital visits?

We perform daily automated and manual schedule reconciliation. Our team compares provider appointment logs, facility census records, and EHR signed note lists against entered billing batches to flag and capture unbilled appointments.

Can Priority Sure handle charge entry in our existing PM/EHR software?

Yes. Our certified billing teams work directly within your practice management software (e.g., Epic, eClinicalWorks, Kareo, AdvancedMD, Athenahealth) via secure, encrypted access without requiring data migration.
