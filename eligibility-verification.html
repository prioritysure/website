Transforming Front-Desk Workflows into High-Performing Revenue Gateways

In high-deductible health plan (HDHP) environments, patient cost-sharing now accounts for up to 30% of a practice's total revenue. Collecting these funds after a patient leaves the clinic is costly and inefficient, often leading to uncollectable bad debt write-offs.

Priority Sure closes this gap by converting patient eligibility verification into a proactive pre-service strategy. We audit every patient appointment across 20+ financial data points, enabling your front desk to communicate clear cost expectations and collect co-pays, deductibles, and co-insurance at the time of check-in.

Eligibility Verification FAQs

How does Priority Sure calculate patient cost estimates for complex procedures?

We load your practice's contracted payer fee schedules into our eligibility engine. By matching anticipated procedure CPT codes against the patient's remaining deductible and co-insurance terms, we compute highly accurate out-of-pocket financial estimates.

How are same-day or walk-in patient eligibility checks handled?

Our real-time EDI integration allows your front-office staff to trigger on-demand eligibility checks through your Practice Management system, receiving verified status and copay responses within seconds.

What happens if a patient has secondary or tertiary insurance coverage?

Our team verifies active status across primary, secondary, and tertiary plans, establishing proper Coordination of Benefits (COB) order to prevent claim rejections caused by improper filing sequence.
