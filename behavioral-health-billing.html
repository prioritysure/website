Mastering the Nuances of Mental Health & Addiction Billing

Behavioral health medical billing is one of the most volatile areas in healthcare revenue cycle management. Payers enforce strict medical necessity reviews, complex concurrent authorization schedules, daily unit caps, and rigid rules surrounding time-based CPT codes (e.g., 90834 vs. 90837 psychotherapy).

Whether operating an outpatient psychotherapy practice, a community mental health center (CMHC), or an Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP) / Partial Hospitalization Program (PHP), Priority Sure delivers specialized billing strategies. We ensure full compliance with the Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act (MHPAEA), prevent gaps in authorization, and maximize reimbursement.

Behavioral Health Billing Quick Reference

Key coding and authorization rules across different behavioral health care settings.

Level of Care / Service Primary CPT / Revenue Codes Authorization & Billing Requirements
Outpatient Psychotherapy CPT 90832 (30 min), 90834 (45 min), 90837 (60 min) Exact time-tracking required in clinical note; monitor payer frequency caps per week.
Psychiatric Diagnostic Eval CPT 90791 (Psych), 90792 (Psych with Med Services) Billed at onset of care or annually; requires comprehensive biopsychosocial assessment.
Intensive Outpatient (IOP) Rev Code 0905 / CPT H0015 (3+ hours per day) Prior authorization mandatory; require weekly concurrent review summaries for continuation.
Partial Hospitalization (PHP) Rev Code 0912, 0913 / CPT H2012 (20+ hours per week) Strict medical necessity oversight; daily multidisciplinary care documentation required.
Telepsychiatry & Telehealth CPT Codes with Modifier 95 / GT (POS 02 or POS 10) Verify state parity mandates and payer-specific telehealth origin site regulations.

Behavioral Health Billing FAQs

How do you prevent denials for 60-minute psychotherapy (90837)?

Commercial payers often audit 90837 claims. Priority Sure ensures your clinical documentation clearly reflects exact start/stop times, clinical complexity, and medical necessity to defend against downcoding to 90834.

What is the difference between Behavioral Health and Mental Health billing?

Behavioral health is a broader umbrella covering mental health conditions as well as substance use disorders (SUD), addiction medicine, eating disorders, and higher levels of care like PHP and IOP.

How does Priority Sure manage concurrent authorizations for PHP/IOP?

Our dedicated authorization team tracks authorized unit counts daily, alerting clinicians days in advance to submit updated clinical notes, UR summaries, and treatment plan extensions before authorized visits run out.
