Navigating Complexities in Outpatient & Telehealth Psychiatry

Mental health reimbursement requires meticulous adherence to strict time-based CPT documentation guidelines, mid-level provider billing restrictions, and evolving telehealth rules. Audits targeting 60-minute psychotherapy sessions (CPT 90837) vs. 45-minute sessions (CPT 90834) frequently result in severe financial recoupments if start and stop times, medical necessity, or interactive complexity (+90785) are improperly documented.

Additionally, distinguishing medical evaluation and management (E/M 99212–99215) with psychiatric add-on codes (+90833, +90836, +90838) requires specialized coding expertise that general billers often lack. Priority Sure LLC provides dedicated mental health billing specialists who ensure full compliance with the Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act (MHPAEA), Medicare telehealth Place of Service rules (POS 02 vs. POS 10), and modifier usage (95, GT, FQ, FR).

Mental Health Coding Quick Reference

Key procedural CPT codes, time thresholds, and mandatory documentation requirements.

CPT Code Service Description Time Range & Documentation Rule
90791 / 90792 Psychiatric Diagnostic Evaluation (without / with medical services) Replaces old intake codes. Billed once per episode of care unless significant clinical change occurs.
90832 Psychotherapy, 30 minutes Actual time span: 16 to 37 minutes. Requires documented face-to-face start and stop times.
90834 Psychotherapy, 45 minutes Actual time span: 38 to 52 minutes. Core standard session for most outpatient therapy.
90837 Psychotherapy, 60 minutes Actual time span: 53+ minutes. High target for commercial audits; requires clear medical justification.
+90833 / +90836 Psychotherapy Add-On (30m / 45m) with E/M Service Billed by MDs/NPs alongside E/M codes (99213–99215). Psychotherapy and E/M time must be separately logged.
90839 / +90840 Psychotherapy for Crisis (First 60 mins / Each additional 30 mins) Requires documented high-acuity crisis, immediate danger, or acute complex stress resolution.

Mental Health Billing FAQs

What is the difference between Place of Service (POS) 02 and POS 10 for telepsychiatry?

POS 02 indicates telehealth provided in a facility location other than the patient's home, while POS 10 indicates telehealth provided in the patient's home. Submitting incorrect POS codes can cause immediate claim rejections under updated CMS and commercial rules.

Why do payers frequently audit 60-minute psychotherapy sessions (CPT 90837)?

Because 90837 yields higher reimbursement rates, insurance payers monitor utilization ratios. Claims require exact documented start and stop times (e.g., 53+ minutes) and clear notes explaining why a prolonged session was clinically necessary.

Can Licensed Clinical Social Workers (LCSWs) and LPCs bill incident-to a psychiatrist?

In Medicare, licensed therapists cannot bill incident-to for direct therapy because therapy has its own benefit category; they must bill directly under their own NPI. Commercial rules vary, and our credentialing specialists ensure compliant setup.
