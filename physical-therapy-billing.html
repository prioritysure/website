Navigating the Complexities of Physical Therapy Reimbursement

Physical therapy medical billing requires precise adherence to rules that do not exist in general medical billing. From distinguishing between Medicare’s 8-minute rule and commercial AMA rule calculations to applying mandatory therapy modifiers (GP for physical therapy, GO for occupational therapy, CQ/CO for assistant-rendered services), even minor errors lead to claim rejections or costly post-payment audits.

At Priority Sure LLC, our AAPC-certified billing team specializes in rehabilitation RCM. We audit documentation for total timed minutes, monitor annual Medicare therapy thresholds for KX modifier application, track commercial visit limitations, and manage authorization renewals so your therapists can stay focused on patient outcomes.

Physical Therapy Billing FAQs

What is the difference between the 8-minute rule and AMA billing rules?

Medicare uses the cumulative 8-minute rule where total timed minutes determine total units. Most commercial payers follow AMA guidelines, where each individual code must cross the 8-minute threshold independently. Priority Sure configures rule sets per payer to maximize compliant billing.

When is the KX modifier required for physical therapy claims?

The KX modifier is required on Medicare Part B claims once a beneficiary’s incurred therapy expenses exceed the annual threshold. It attests that additional services remain medically necessary and are fully documented in the chart.

How do you handle denials for missing Plan of Care (POC) physician signatures?

We implement pre-claim submission checks to verify that signed POCs are uploaded within 30 days of initial evaluation. If a denial occurs, our A/R team obtains retroactive certification letters and files targeted appeals.
