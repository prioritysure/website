Why Precise Payment Posting is the Heart of Revenue Cycle Health

Payment posting is far more than routine data entry—it is the primary diagnostic center of your revenue cycle. Rapid posting ensures patient balances are current, secondary insurance claims are dispatched without delay, and unpaid claim denials are instantly surfaced for appeal.

Inaccurate or delayed posting distorts practice financial reports, triggers inappropriate patient billing statements, delays secondary claims, and allows zero-pay denials to sit unnoticed until timely filing windows pass. Priority Sure delivers rigorous, double-checked payment posting workflows that give practice leadership total financial peace of mind.

Our 4-Stage Payment Posting Workflow

A rigorous process engineered to ensure total audit control and zero posting discrepancies.

Step Action Items Financial Outcome
1. Remittance Retrieval Downloading ERA files from clearinghouses and scanning paper EOBs/lockbox files into payment processing queues. Centralized tracking of 100% inbound remittance files.
2. Line-Item Posting & Write-Offs Posting payments, contractual adjustments, deductibles, co-pays, and non-covered amounts to matching line items. Accurate patient ledgers and true contractual allowance tracking.
3. Denial Escalation & Crossover Flagging zero-pay codes for denial team follow-up while auto-dispatching secondary claims with attached primary EOBs. Sub-24 hour routing of denied claims and faster secondary cash flow.

Payment Posting FAQs

How does Priority Sure reconcile EFT bank deposits with ERA files?

Our posting specialists perform daily three-way reconciliations comparing your bank EFT deposit notices, clearinghouse 835 ERAs, and PM system postings to ensure every dollar deposited matches a posted patient account.

What happens when a payment is posting as a partial payment or zero-pay denial?

Denied or underpaid line items are immediately tagged using standard CARC/RARC reason codes and electronically transferred to our denial management specialists within 24 hours for immediate appeal creation.

Can you process paper EOBs and physical checks from a bank lockbox?

Yes. We securely connect to your bank lockbox portal or scanned document repository to index and manually post paper EOBs, check stubs, and patient payments with sub-24 hour turnaround times.
