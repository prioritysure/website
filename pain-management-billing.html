Mastering the Complexities of Interventional Pain Reimbursement

Interventional pain management is subject to some of the strictest payer audits, Local Coverage Determinations (LCDs), and billing guidelines in healthcare. Modern CPT code definitions for epidurals (62320–62323), transforaminal injections (64479–64484), and facet joint interventions (64490–64495) bundle fluoroscopic or CT guidance (77002/77003) directly into the primary procedure code. Billing imaging separately results in immediate denials or post-payment clawbacks.

Furthermore, major commercial payers and Medicare Administrative Contractors (MACs) impose stringent prior authorization rules for spinal cord stimulator (SCS) trials and implants, radiofrequency ablation (RFA), and definitive urine drug testing (UDT G0480–G0483). Priority Sure LLC’s certified AAPC coding experts verify conservative care history, ensure precise anatomical modifier usage (50, RT, LT), and manage facility vs. professional fee splits seamlessly.

Pain Management Billing FAQs

Can fluoroscopic guidance (CPT 77002 or 77003) ever be billed separately with epidurals?

No. Current CPT injection codes (e.g., 62321, 62323, 64479, 64490) explicitly include fluoroscopic or CT imaging guidance. Unbundling fluoroscopy is a primary target of Medicare RAC and CERT audits.

What documentation is required to secure prior authorization for Spinal Cord Stimulators (SCS)?

Payers require documented failure of conservative therapies (physical therapy, pharmacotherapy, injections), an official psychological clearance report, and detailed clinical notes establishing severe neuropathic pain.

How does Priority Sure handle bilateral facet injections or radiofrequency ablation?

We append Modifier 50 or RT/LT according to specific commercial and Medicare MAC guidelines, adjusting charge units correctly to ensure full 150% bilateral reimbursement without trigger denials.
