Overcoming the Complexities of Pre-Certification

Prior authorization requirements continue to expand across commercial payers, Medicare Advantage, and Medicaid programs. Obtaining approvals for imaging, surgical procedures, specialty drugs, and physical therapy demands hours of administrative follow-up, pulling medical staff away from direct patient care.

Priority Sure takes total ownership of your prior authorization pipeline. From gathering clinical records and completing payer-specific forms to tracking real-time portal status and coordinating peer-to-peer reviews, we ensure approvals are secured long before patient appointments take place.

Our 4-Step Prior Authorization Workflow

A systematic framework designed to secure insurance approvals fast and keep patient care on schedule.

Stage Operational Actions Expected Output
1. Trigger & Intake Order identification in EHR, CPT/ICD-10 verification, and payer auth requirement lookup. Auth necessity confirmed within 2 hours of scheduling.
2. Clinical Package Assembly Extraction of provider notes, Conservative Care documentation, prior imaging, and clinical rationale. Audit-proof submission packet assembled.
3. Submission & Active Tracking Electronic submission via portal/EDI and daily follow-up calls to payer medical management units. Real-time status updates logged in EHR.
4. Approval & EHR Attach Obtaining formal auth number, approved unit counts, and attaching determination letter to encounter notes. Claim cleared for clean charge entry and billing.

Prior Authorization FAQs

How does Priority Sure access our clinical records to submit prior authorizations?

Our authorized team securely accesses your Electronic Health Record (EHR) via encrypted, role-based credentials. We extract required clinical notes, lab tests, or imaging reports directly without disrupting your clinical workflow.

What happens if an initial prior authorization request is denied?

If a payer issues a denial, our specialists review the denial reason immediately. We coordinate additional clinical documentation, file an expedited appeal, or schedule a Peer-to-Peer discussion for your physician.

How far in advance of a patient visit do you require order details?

For elective procedures, we recommend 3 to 5 business days ahead of time. However, for urgent cases, our Stat Authorization team can initiate and secure approvals within 4 to 24 hours depending on the payer.
