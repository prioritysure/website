Unlocking Revenue Trapped in Aging Payer Buckets

Uncollected claims are one of the single largest threats to medical practice stability. Insurance payers frequently stall claims through request for information (RFI) loops, lost documentation claims, or improper processing, hoping the practice will miss strict timely filing deadlines.

At Priority Sure, our medical A/R specialists do not passively wait for Remittance Advices. We deploy structured, bucket-by-bucket audit protocols, directly engaging payer representatives via phone, electronic portals, and supervisor escalations to resolve outstanding claims before they cross timely filing thresholds.

Accounts Receivable FAQs

What is considered an ideal "Days in A/R" target for a medical practice?

An ideal benchmark is under 35 days, with high-performing practices maintaining between 28 and 32 days. Anything above 45 days indicates severe bottlenecks in billing, posting, or follow-up.

Can Priority Sure clean up an existing backlog of old accounts receivable?

Yes. We offer specialized A/R project cleanup services to work through existing aging backlogs (60, 90, 120+ days) and liquidate uncollected claims while your staff focuses on ongoing daily operations.

How do you handle claims approaching timely filing deadlines?

Our system automatically flags claims approaching 60% of their timely filing window. We re-verify original electronic EDI acceptance reports and resubmit claims with explicit proof of timely initial filing to prevent uncollectible write-offs.
